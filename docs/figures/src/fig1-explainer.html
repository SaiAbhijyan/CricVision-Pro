How the app finds the exact frame where bat meets ball

A camera is too slow to catch the instant of contact on its own. The sound of the hit tells it when to look.

1

The camera keeps a rolling memory

It stores recent frames and drops the oldest as new ones arrive.

2

The ball hits the bat: a loud crack

The microphone listens at 48,000 samples a second.

3

The app spots the spike

A hit that is sharp and bright is a bat. A dull thud is the pad.

4

It picks the frame from time t

That frame is the contact moment, shown with the pose and shot.

Why it matters: the app can show you where your head, hands and weight were at the instant of contact, not a blurry guess near it. Phone microphones add a small delay, so the app lets you tune it per device.
